Raising children in Müggelsee is genuinely good — the lake, the forest, the relative quiet — but it helps to know other families in the same situation. This group is for parents with young children who want to meet up, share information about local schools and nurseries, and occasionally organise things together.

Meetings tend to happen at playgrounds and parks where children can run around while adults talk.
