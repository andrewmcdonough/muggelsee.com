Working from home in Müggelsee means you can take a proper lunch break. This group does exactly that — meets midweek at a local café or restaurant, usually between noon and 1:30pm.

It's informal. Sometimes four people show up, sometimes twelve. The point is to get out of the house and eat with other humans.
