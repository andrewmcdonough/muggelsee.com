The area is genuinely excellent for outdoor exercise — forest running trails, open water swimming, long flat cycling routes. This group coordinates people who want to do those things together rather than alone.

Activities vary by season and by whoever shows up. In summer it skews toward swimming and cycling. In winter, running and the occasional cold-water dip for the brave ones.
