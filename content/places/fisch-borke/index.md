A genuine highlight of the area. Fisch-Borke sits right on the lake, and whether you're inside at the cosy tables arranged around a central fire (with windows framing the water) or out on the terrace soaking up the views, the setting is hard to beat. The expansive green outside area is dotted with deckchairs facing the lake, and regular evenings of live music make this a real local event venue.

The menu leans heavily into fish, as you'd expect, but there's usually at least one meat option (the steak has been excellent) and solid vegetarian choices too. Families are particularly well catered for: the kids' menu features home-made fish nuggets that are genuinely impressive, and a small play corner with toys keeps little ones occupied. It's worth noting the food isn't quite what it was at opening, but it remains tasty. Prices are on the higher side.
