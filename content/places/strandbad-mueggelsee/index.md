Berlin's largest inland beach, and one of its best. Strandbad Müggelsee has been here since 1906 in various forms, and on a hot summer day it fills up with a genuinely mixed crowd — families, older locals, cyclists who've come out from the city.

The water quality is consistently good. There's a proper changing area, a café, and enough space that you can usually find a quiet patch of sand even when it's busy. Worth arriving before noon in high summer.
