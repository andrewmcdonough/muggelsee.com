The Müggelturm is an observation tower at the highest point of the Müggelberge — not high by any standard outside Berlin, but enough to give you a proper view over the lake and forest. The current tower dates from 1961; there's been some kind of structure here since the 1880s.

The walk up through the forest is the best part. There are several marked trails from Rahnsdorf and from the Rübezahl ferry landing. The café at the top is functional rather than special, but the view earns it.
