Rübezahl sits right at the water's edge on the south shore of Müggelsee, accessible by the small passenger ferry from Rahnsdorf or by bike through the forest. The setting alone makes it worth the trip — you eat outside looking across the lake.

The food is straightforward German café fare. What you're really paying for is the location and the fact that you had to make a small effort to get here.
