Berlin's best-kept secret is right at the end of the S3. Müggelsee and the villages around it — Rahnsdorf, Köpenick, Müggelheim, and across the water to Erkner — offer something rare for a city this size: real forests, a proper lake, and a community that actually knows its neighbours.

This site is a guide for people who live here, have just moved here, or are thinking about making the journey east. You'll find the best places to swim, eat, walk, and work, plus a way to meet others who've chosen this corner of Berlin.
