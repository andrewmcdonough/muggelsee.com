## 2. What Data We Collect

When you subscribe to our community mailing list, we collect the following personal data:

- Your name
- Your email address

We do not collect any other personal data through this website. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

---

## 3. Why We Collect It and Our Legal Basis

We collect your name and email address for the following purposes:

- To send you updates about community events in and around the Muggelsee area of east Berlin
- To send you local news relevant to the community
- To notify you of updates and changes to this website

Our legal basis for processing your personal data is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR). You provide this consent when you actively sign up to our mailing list. You may withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing (see Section 7 below).

---

## 4. How Long We Keep Your Data

We will retain your name and email address for as long as you remain subscribed to our mailing list. If you unsubscribe or request deletion of your data, we will remove your information from our records within 30 days.

---

## 5. Third Party Services

**Email service provider**

To send our community emails, we use a third party email service provider. Your name and email address are shared with this provider solely for the purpose of delivering emails on our behalf. We will update this policy with the name of the provider before launching the mailing list.

**WhatsApp Community Groups**

This website may display QR codes or links that allow you to join community WhatsApp groups. If you choose to scan a QR code or click a link to join a WhatsApp group, you will be directed to WhatsApp, a service operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited.

We do not collect, store or process your phone number or any other data when you use these QR codes. Any personal data you share within WhatsApp groups is governed by WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy, available at [whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy). We encourage you to review it before joining.

---

## 6. Who Else Has Access to Your Data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is only shared with our email service provider as described in Section 5, and only to the extent necessary to deliver our community emails.

We may disclose your data if required to do so by law or by a competent authority.

## 7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

- The right to access the personal data we hold about you
- The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- The right to erasure (the right to be forgotten)
- The right to restrict processing of your data
- The right to data portability
- The right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal
- The right to object to processing

To exercise any of these rights, or to unsubscribe from our mailing list, please contact us at contact@muggelsee.com. We will respond to all requests within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk). In Germany, this is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit (BlnBDI) at [datenschutz-berlin.de](https://www.datenschutz-berlin.de).
