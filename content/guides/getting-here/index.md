## By S-Bahn

The S3 is the lifeline. It runs from central Berlin (Ostbahnhof, Alexanderplatz, Ostkreuz) to Erkner, stopping at Köpenick and Rahnsdorf along the way. Journey time from Ostkreuz to Rahnsdorf is about 25 minutes.

Trains run frequently during the day, less so in the early morning and late evening.

## By Tram

From Köpenick S-Bahn station, tram lines connect to various parts of the area. The 68 runs south towards Müggelheim.

## By Bike

Cycling from central Berlin is very doable — there are dedicated cycle paths along much of the route, and the flat terrain through Treptow and along the Spree makes it pleasant. Allow 1.5–2 hours from Mitte.

## By Ferry

In summer, a small passenger ferry crosses between Rahnsdorf and the south shore of the lake, connecting to Rübezahl and the Müggelberge. It runs approximately April to October.

## By Car

Parking is available in most areas, though it fills up quickly on summer weekends near the beach. Coming by public transport or bike is strongly recommended on hot days.
