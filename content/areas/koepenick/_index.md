Köpenick is the largest and oldest of the Müggelsee-area settlements, with a proper old town on a small island at the point where the Dahme meets the Spree. The palace is worth seeing; the surrounding water is even better.

It's also where you'll find most of the practical amenities — supermarkets, a hospital, government offices — while still being surrounded by water on three sides.
