Erkner sits just over the Berlin-Brandenburg border, close enough to feel connected to the city but with its own quieter rhythm. The town centre is small and unpretentious, and the surrounding lakes and forest make it a popular destination for cyclists and swimmers coming from the west.

Gerhart Hauptmann lived here, and there's a museum dedicated to him that's worth an hour if you're interested in German literature.
