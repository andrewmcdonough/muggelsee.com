Woltersdorf is just across the Berlin–Brandenburg state line, connected to Rahnsdorf and Friedrichshagen by the Woltersdorfer Straßenbahn — a heritage tram that has been running since 1913. It's a short ride but it feels like a different pace.

The town sits on the Flakensee and Dämeritzsee chain of lakes. The Schleuse Woltersdorf — a manually operated boat lock linking the lakes to the Oder–Spree Canal — is worth a stop. Boats wait, the lock fills, and nobody seems to be in much of a hurry.
