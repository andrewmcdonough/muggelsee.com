Rahnsdorf sits at the eastern tip of Müggelsee, where the lake narrows into the Müggelspree. It's one of Berlin's few remaining villages that still feels like one — narrow lanes, weekend allotments, a proper harbour, and a beach that gets genuinely busy in summer.

The S3 stops here, which makes it accessible but not overrun. Most people walk straight to the water; the quieter paths through the forest behind the village are worth finding.
