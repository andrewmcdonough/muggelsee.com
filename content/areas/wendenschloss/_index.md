Wendenschloss sits between Köpenick and the Müggelsee, on the southern shore of the Langer See. It's a quiet residential area — detached houses behind old trees, pontoons, small sailing clubs — with a waterfront path that makes for an easy and pleasant walk or cycle.

The area has stayed largely low-key despite its location, which is most of its appeal. Good access to the forest on one side and the lake chain on the other.
