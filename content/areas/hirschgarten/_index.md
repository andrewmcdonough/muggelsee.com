Hirschgarten is a small, quiet area tucked between the eastern edge of Rahnsdorf and the Berlin state border. It's mostly forest and the occasional allotment garden — the kind of place that doesn't ask much of you.

Good for cycling through on the way to Erkner or Woltersdorf, or as a quiet alternative to the busier stretches of lakeside path.
