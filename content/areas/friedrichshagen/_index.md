Friedrichshagen sits on the north shore of the Müggelsee and has been a resort destination since the nineteenth century. The Bölschestraße — one of Berlin's most intact Wilhelmine-era streets — runs from the S-Bahn straight down to the lake, lined with cafés, bakeries and independent shops.

The waterfront promenade is the main event: a long stretch of lake shore with benches, sailing clubs, and the Strandbad Friedrichshagen lido at the eastern end. It's the most accessible part of the Müggelsee by public transport and feels properly lived-in rather than touristy.
