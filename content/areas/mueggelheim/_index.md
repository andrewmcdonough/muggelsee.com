Müggelheim is the southernmost of the lakeside villages and the hardest to reach without a bike or car. That's part of what makes it special. The village itself is tiny, but it sits at the edge of the Köpenick Forest — one of the largest urban forests in Europe.

If you want to feel genuinely far from Berlin without actually being far from Berlin, this is the place.
