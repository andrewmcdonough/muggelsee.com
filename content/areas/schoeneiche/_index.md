Schöneiche bei Berlin is a quiet residential municipality southeast of the lake, served by the historic Schöneiche–Rüdersdorf tram — one of the oldest narrow-gauge tram lines still running in Germany. It connects to Friedrichshagen, making the whole southeastern lakeside area surprisingly easy to navigate without a car.

The area has a low-key, village-suburb character: detached houses, allotment gardens, and easy access to the forests that stretch between here and Müggelheim.
